Transparent by design

How SpendSense uses AI

AI never touches arithmetic or invents balances. Code calculates the facts; Gemini explains them in plain language.

Step 1 · Deterministic code

Numbers are calculated in code

Your safe-to-spend balance, runway days, budget percentages, and currency conversions are computed server-side using exact integer minor units — zero floating-point guesses.

Step 2 · Grounded AI

AI explains, never invents

Money Buddy only receives your computed snapshot summary. If the AI service is offline or busy, SpendSense falls back seamlessly to grounded offline responses.

Step 3 · Inspectable working

Every answer shows its working

Open "Why this answer?" in Money Buddy or "How we calculated this" in Before You Spend to inspect the exact figures behind every suggestion.

Privacy & what we store

Personal accounts store only your email, Argon2id-hashed password, theme and currency preferences, transactions, budgets, goals, and an optional EXIF-stripped avatar. Temporary demo accounts auto-expire, payment screenshots are parsed in memory without saving raw images, and your data is never sold or used to train models. You can delete your account and all data anytime from Profile.
